# Overview

This section is designed to help translate the technical work completed throughout this portfolio into confident, structured interview responses.

Building a strong QA portfolio is only part of the journey. The ability to clearly explain testing decisions, communicate business impact, and demonstrate problem-solving skills is equally important during interviews.

This folder provides structured interview preparation materials covering behavioural, technical, domain-specific, and practical QA scenarios.

---

The objectives of this section are to help candidates:

* Explain testing decisions clearly.
* Demonstrate business understanding.
* Answer technical questions effectively.
* Discuss automation strategy.
* Present portfolio projects professionally.
* Handle real-world debugging scenarios.
* Showcase quality engineering thinking.

# Recommended Interview Preparation Routine

To build confidence, practise regularly:

### Daily (30–45 minutes)

* Review technical questions.
* Solve SQL exercises.
* Explain one testing concept aloud.
* Review one STAR story.

### Weekly

* Present the portfolio to a friend or mentor.
* Complete a mock technical interview.
* Practise explaining a recent defect investigation.
* Review one fintech topic in depth.

---

# Interview Mindset

Successful interviews are not about memorising answers.

Instead, focus on demonstrating that you can:

* Understand business requirements.
* Think critically.
* Assess risk.
* Design effective tests.
* Investigate problems systematically.
* Communicate clearly.
* Learn from feedback.
* Collaborate with development and product teams.
